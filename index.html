Who we are

A student team where mechanical grit meets code, circuits, and match-day pressure.

Team RoboManipal is the official robotics team of Manipal University Jaipur, established in 2017. We are a multidisciplinary team of 20 undergraduate robotics enthusiasts who endeavour to achieve innovation through robotic technologies and spread knowledge about its diverse applications. Our legacy is built on building, testing, competing, researching, and learning together.

How we build

Design. Fabricate. Wire. Code. Break. Tune. Repeat.

The team works like a small engineering pit crew: CAD and fabrication on one side, embedded code and controls on another, with testing tying it all together.

01

Mechanical

Chassis, mounts, drivetrain layouts, repair access, and impact tolerance.

02

Electronics

Power systems, motor drivers, sensors, wiring discipline, and diagnostics.

03

Software

Control loops, embedded logic, computer vision, telemetry, and strategy tools.

04

Operations

Match planning, documentation, spare parts, pit workflow, and competition logistics.
