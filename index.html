ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Dhairya.

At Manipal University Jaipur, I study Electronics and Communication Engineering. I like projects that give me something to test with my own hands. A line-following robot has a wonderfully blunt way of telling you when your code is wrong.

That’s what connects my work on motor drivers, piston inspection and websites. I get to follow an idea from a sketch to the part where someone can actually use it.
